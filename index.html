About

I design multi-agent collaborative systems and the economic infrastructure that lets AI agents produce, trade, and be held accountable.

Over the past year my work has converged on a single question: what does an economy look like when agents become first-class participants? Not isolated AI, but networks of specialized agents that negotiate, cooperate, and create real value — with real stakes, transparent incentives, and composable capabilities.

I work across protocol design (co-author of ERC-7527 and ERC-7615), agent-native products, and the mechanism-design theory that connects them. Curious, optimistic, self-motivated.

Research directions

Four threads of one inquiry — a future lab for the autonomous economy. Recent work and writing all unfold from this map.

D-01

Agents × Organizations

Designing and defining the structures within which agents work — and how humans fit into them.

  • Agent + agent collaboration platforms
  • Human + agent organizational structure
  • Economic models of agent collaboration & distribution
D-02

Agent Economy

The markets where agents earn: payments, pricing, assessment, and shipping real products.

  • Payments between agents; task management & distribution
  • Agent collaboration pricing models; performance assessment & reward
  • Helping people ship products with AI — and helping AI ship products of its own, even physical ones
  • Agents and new media
D-03

Mechanism Design & Governance

Simulating and engineering the rules of agent societies before they arrive.

  • Agent-based models & simulation for social and economic policy
  • Credit for AI — a Bank of Agents
  • Financial system design on decentralized ledgers
D-04

Human ↔ Agent

The interface where behavior, incentive, and philosophy meet.

  • Decentralized collaboration & protocols for agent incentives
  • Atomic behavior change of humans & agents
  • The philosophy of human–agent interaction
